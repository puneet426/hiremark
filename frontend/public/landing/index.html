Is my camera recorded?

No. Your browser checks that one face is in frame. No video or image is uploaded. We keep the transcript, your code and the integrity log.

How is the score decided?

By code. Hidden tests, the timer, hints and warnings are measured. Spoken answers are checked against fixed key points.

Who can see my result?

Only you, until you publish it. Recruiters see your contact details only after you approve their request.

Can I retake the interview?

Yes. The leaderboard shows your latest result and how many attempts you took.

What do I need?

A laptop with Chrome, a camera, a microphone and forty quiet minutes.
